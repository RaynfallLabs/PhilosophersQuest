# Geography strategy taxonomy

The geography bank is a **guided tour of the beautiful world we live in**. Place names are the gateway to wonder; every question links a real place to a real magical fact — an ancient building, a natural marvel, a cultural birthplace, an earth-process that makes the place uniquely itself.

The bank teaches kids three things in priority order:

1. **The world is vast and varied** — every continent, every climate, every civilization carries its own wonders
2. **Place names are portals** — Petra, Bukhara, Cusco, Lhasa, Iguazu, Baikal should arrive in their ears already glowing
3. **Geography is the meeting ground of history + science + culture** — the bank is relentlessly cross-linked

**Wonder over utility.** Strategic / chokepoint / supply-chain framing belongs elsewhere. This bank exists to make kids want to GO somewhere.

Five pillars:

1. **Cities of wonder** — Paris, Vienna, Istanbul, Kyoto, Petra, Marrakech, Cusco, Bukhara, Lhasa, Reykjavik, Prague, Granada, Samarkand, etc. — each city carries multiple hooks: architecture, music, food, history, hidden marvels
2. **Natural wonders** — Iguazu Falls, Lake Baikal, the Aurora Borealis, Mount Everest, the Galapagos, the Sahara, the Amazon, Yellowstone, Mariana Trench, Antarctica
3. **Ancient marvels & lost civilizations** — Giza, Angkor, Machu Picchu, Stonehenge, Easter Island, Pompeii, Persepolis, Chichen Itza, Petra, the Great Wall, Cahokia, Great Zimbabwe
4. **Cultural birthplaces** — Waltz/Vienna, Tango/Buenos Aires, Jazz/New Orleans, Tea/Fujian, Pizza/Naples, Coffee/Yemen-Ethiopia, Origami/Japan, Sushi/Edo, Chocolate/Mesoamerica, Curry/Indian subcontinent
5. **Magical earth systems** — Mid-Atlantic Ridge under Iceland, why Madagascar's animals are unique, why the Atacama is the driest place, monsoon mechanics, Aurora science, Yellowstone supervolcano, plate tectonics through place names

Every question carries `_meta.strategy` + `_meta.strategy_pillar`. Target: ≥500 questions per tier; total **3000-3500 final keepers**.

## Stance summary

| Topic | Stance |
|---|---|
| Age of Discovery / Western exploration | CELEBRATED — Magellan, Cook, Lewis & Clark, Mercator, Marco Polo |
| Colonialism | HONEST — both costs and benefits; no one-sided framing |
| Western civilization's reach | CELEBRATED — Roman roads, Christendom, English lingua franca |
| Climate as geography | COVERED descriptively — climate zones, monsoons, El Niño; alarmism avoided |
| Contested borders | NAMED honestly — Taiwan, Crimea/Donbas, Kashmir, Israel/Palestine |
| Indigenous civilizations | CELEBRATED as full civilizations — Inca roads, Mayan astronomy, Polynesian wayfinding |
| Religious sites | RESPECTED — Mecca, Jerusalem, Bodh Gaya, Notre Dame, Hagia Sophia, Angkor Wat |
| Soviet / Maoist legacy on geography | HONEST — Aral Sea, Three Gorges, Great Leap famine, Gulag map |
