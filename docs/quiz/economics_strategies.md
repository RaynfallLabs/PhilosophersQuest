# Economics strategy taxonomy

The economics bank is **substantively Austrian** and treats **Bitcoin (not crypto) as a great human achievement**. Same anti-authoritarian thread as the other banks: central planning fails for the same reason wherever tried (Mises 1920 calculation problem; Hayek knowledge problem); the Federal Reserve + fiat money + Keynesianism + MMT are honest critiques of authoritarian-friendly monetary regimes; Bitcoin solves the political-economic trust problem at the protocol layer.

Three priorities:

1. **The Austrian school is correct** — Menger, Mises, Hayek, Rothbard, Hoppe, Sowell-as-fellow-traveler — substantive coverage, real arguments, real history; vindicated by 20th-century evidence
2. **Bitcoin in depth** — the user's explicit request: a full pillar covering Bitcoin's technical mechanics, history, cypherpunk lineage, monetary economics, and philosophy
3. **Honest critique of authoritarian monetary regimes** — Federal Reserve, fiat money, Keynesianism, MMT presented + critiqued; hyperinflations honestly counted; sound money (gold and Bitcoin) honestly framed

Five pillars:

1. **Austrian school foundations + figures** (~25%) — Menger through modern Austrians
2. **Bitcoin** (~25%) — full pillar: tech, history, ideas, ecosystem
3. **Sound money + hyperinflation history** (~17%) — gold standard, fiat collapses, debasement throughout history
4. **Central banking + Keynes/MMT critique** (~18%) — Fed history, mainstream macro critiqued
5. **Practical economics for kids** (~15%) — Bastiat, comparative advantage, supply/demand, prices, spontaneous order, public choice

Every question carries `_meta.strategy` + `_meta.strategy_pillar`. Target: ≥500 questions per tier; total **3000-3200 final keepers**.
