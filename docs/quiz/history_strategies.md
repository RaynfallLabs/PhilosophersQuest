# History strategy taxonomy

History is the **story of humanity**. The bank teaches kids that real people faced real choices under real pressure, and that what they did shapes the world we live in. Names — Thermopylae, Magna Carta, Trenton, Gettysburg, Sobieski, Wilberforce, Solzhenitsyn, Reagan, Sun Tzu, Akbar, Saladin — should arrive in kids' ears meaning something.

Three priorities:

1. **The Western tradition unbroken** — Greeks → Romans → Christendom → Reformation → Scientific Revolution → Enlightenment → American Founding produced the most successful experiment in human liberty. This gets TOP BILLING.
2. **Communist atrocities of the 20th century, honestly counted** — Lenin's terror, Holodomor 3.5-7M, Great Purge, Gulag, Mao's 30-45M dead, Cultural Revolution, Khmer Rouge, North Korea. Specific death tolls, specific perpetrators. No false equivalence.
3. **Story, not rote** — every question is a scene with named people and real stakes. The anti-rote regex bans the dry stuff at the floor.

Six pillars:

1. **The Western tradition unbroken** — Greeks → Romans → Christendom → Reformation → Scientific Revolution → Enlightenment → Industrial Revolution
2. **The American story** — Revolutionary War (deep), Founders + early republic, Civil War + Reconstruction (deep), through ~2010
3. **Great battles + military history of the world**
4. **20th century truth-telling** — communist atrocities + Cold War as moral conflict (HEAVY)
5. **Non-Western civilizations** — Han, Tang, Islamic Golden Age, Mongols, Mughal, Ottoman, Tokugawa, Aztec, Inca, Polynesian, African kingdoms
6. **Iconic moments across all of history** — Franklin/Mesmer, Galileo, Pasteur, Edison, Curie, Wright Brothers, Bannister, Ali, Hogan/Andre, Tiger '97, MJ's last shot

Every question carries `_meta.strategy` + `_meta.strategy_pillar`. Target: ≥500 questions per tier; total **3000-3500 final keepers**.
