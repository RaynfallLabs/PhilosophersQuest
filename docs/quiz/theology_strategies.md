# Theology + Mythology strategy taxonomy

The bank covers **theology and mythology together** — Christianity, Norse, Greek, and other world traditions as the rich, story-saturated, civilization-shaping forces they are. Top billing goes to Christianity (3/8) because of its **civilizational impact** on the modern world, not because the bank privileges its truth-claims.

Three priorities:

1. **Christianity as the central civilizational force** that built the modern West (Bible narratives + church history + theology + Reformation + saints + reformers), with abuses (Inquisition, indulgences, Borgia papacy, witch trials, wars of religion) covered with the same honesty as the moral + intellectual achievements
2. **Norse + Greek mythology** as the rich storytelling + religious traditions they are — deep coverage of gods, heroes, cosmologies, ritual practices, and the ancient world's relationship to the sacred
3. **Same intellectual honesty for all traditions** — Islam's Golden Age celebrated AND honest about how religious authoritarianism closed it off; ancient Aztec / Greek / Norse practices covered with their evils named; modern cult abuses (Jonestown, Heaven's Gate, Aum Shinrikyo) named

Four pillars:

1. **Christianity** (3/8 ≈ 37.5%) — Bible (OT + NT), church history, theology, sacraments, saints, reformers, denominations, abuses; Reformation as central inflection
2. **Greek mythology + religion** (2/8 ≈ 25%) — Olympians, Titans, heroes, Trojan War, Homer + Hesiod, mysteries (Eleusinian, Orphic, Dionysian), oracles, philosophical evolution, religious practices
3. **Norse mythology + religion** (2/8 ≈ 25%) — Aesir + Vanir, Nine Worlds, Yggdrasil, Eddas (Prose + Poetic), sagas, runes, blot rituals, Ragnarok, Viking-age religion
4. **Other religions + world mythologies** (1/8 ≈ 12.5%) — Judaism (post-biblical), Islam, Buddhism, Hinduism, Sikhism, Confucianism, Taoism, Egyptian, Mesopotamian, Celtic, Slavic, Aztec, Maya, Japanese Shinto, Polynesian, African

Every question carries `_meta.strategy` + `_meta.strategy_pillar`. Target: ≥500 questions per tier; total **3000-3200 final keepers**.
