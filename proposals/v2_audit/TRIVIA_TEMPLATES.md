# Trivia Templates (v2)

Per-tier approved stem patterns + anti-patterns. Companion to `TRIVIA_FRAMEWORK.md` (the WHY); this is the WHAT.

Authoring rule: every new trivia question should match an approved template AND fit somewhere on the Easter Egg Pattern hierarchy (`TRIVIA_FRAMEWORK.md` §1). Make them want to **GO SEEK OUT** the source.

**SPOILER POLICY**: 10 named franchises permit full plot spoilers (My Hero Academia, Hajime no Ippo, Harry Potter, Star Wars OT, MCU through Endgame, Toilet-Bound Hanako Kun, Dragon Ball, Scott Pilgrim, Princess Bride, Mario Movie 2023). Everything else: cultural-osmosis carve-out — common-knowledge canon testable, specific plot reveals BANNED.

## §2 Choice-shape conventions

### Length parity

Trivia IS in `ANSWER_OUTLIER_SUBJECTS`. The 1.6× answer-outlier flex applies — a longer Easter-egg answer can legitimately exceed distractor lengths by up to 1.6×.

Distractors should be in approximate parity with each other (1.30 max/min ratio).

### Dash-shape parity

All four choices share dash usage (all use em-dash, or none). Skim-tell failure.

### Distractor plausibility

- **Cast / actors**: distractors are other real actors who plausibly could have been cast in the era. For Akira → distractors include other late-80s anime directors (Miyazaki, Oshii, Otomo himself for misdirection if not the answer)
- **Years**: real adjacent years for the franchise (1979/1980/1981 around Pac-Man's 1980)
- **Characters**: other real adjacent characters from same show / era (Pikachu vs Bulbasaur/Squirtle/Charmander — the other Kanto starters)
- **Modules + settings**: other real TSR modules of the era (B2 Borderlands vs B1 Search/B4 Lost City/T1 Hommlet)
- **Cryptid sightings**: other real cryptid events/photographs (Patterson-Gimlin Oct 20 1967 vs other real Bigfoot dates)
- **High scores**: real adjacent scores (Mitchell 1,047,200 vs Wiebe 1,049,100 vs Wiebe's earlier 1,006,600 vs Mitchell's earlier 874,300)
- **Hidden levels / glitches**: real adjacent glitches (Minus World vs Warp Zone in 1-2 vs Goomba-stomp infinite vs the Princess-not-here bug)

Banned distractor types:
- Made-up actors / titles / years / modules
- "Obvious dumb" or joke distractors that don't test anything
- Modern-multiverse-era distractors mixed with classic content
- Spoiler-bait distractors for non-allowed franchises (forcing the kid to recognize "this is the spoiler")

## §3 Anti-patterns (do not generate)

See `TRIVIA_FRAMEWORK.md` §6. Key points:
- No spoilers OUTSIDE the 10 allowed franchises (cultural-osmosis carve-out is generous)
- No modern multiverse capeshit (Disney+ MCU shows, post-Endgame MCU films, modern X-Men runs)
- No Disney-era Star Wars (sequels VII/VIII/IX, Mandalorian, Andor, Ahsoka)
- No post-Attitude-Era wrestling (post-Hogan-Rock WM18 March 17, 2002)
- No post-Legends MtG (post-June 1994)
- No modern D&D errata (ancestry rewrites, alignment removal, sensitivity reads)
- No credulous cryptid framing OR smug skeptical-dismissive framing (story-led only)
- No bare year-recall as the primary test ("In what year did X come out?" without dramatic context)
- No buried-story pattern (§14) — substance lives in stem
- No weasel closers (§15) — pointed concrete only
- No assumed-knowledge (§16) — foundational figures introduced inline at first appearance

## §4 Per-tier register — fandom-depth scale

Per user 2026-05-27, school-grade level doesn't fit trivia. Tier = fandom depth:

| Tier | Fandom level | Register expectations |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | **Casual cultural knowledge** | What you'd know just from existing in the culture. Pikachu, Mario, Sonic, "Bruce Wayne is Batman", "Luke I am your father", radioactive spider. ≤ 280 total. |
| T2 | **Direct exposure** | What you'd know from actually seeing/playing/reading the thing. Akira's director (Otomo) + 1988, Pac-Man's Iwatani pizza-slice origin, Inigo's full vengeance line. ≤ 480. |
| T3 | **Fan** | What a follower of the franchise would know. Kill-screen levels, pulp-author hometowns, anime-arc-specific moves, cryptid event dates. ≤ 680. |
| T4 | **Huge fan** | Deep cuts — multi-layered named-figure drama (Mitchell vs Wiebe), production-collapse lore (Eva 25/26), Watchmen smiley-face-in-every-issue, classic-module designer + tournament history. ≤ 900. |
| T5 | **Mega fan** | *Ready Player One* deep cuts — Minus World glitch, Princess Bride opening Hardball II Amiga, Akira's Geinoh Yamashirogumi chorus, ABACABB Genesis blood code, Edgar's Double inning detail. ≤ 1100. |

T5 stays accessible — no academic/engineering jargon dump. The point is the kid hears it and immediately wants to find the source.

## §5 The story shape — five-fact reading

A trivia question should reveal these five facts in the stem-and-answer pair:

1. **WHO** — the named figure(s) — Howard, Lovecraft, Iwatani, Miyamoto, Otomo, Mitchell, Wiebe, Sherman, Edgar, Hogan, Garfield, Gygax
2. **WHEN/WHERE** — the named setting — Cross Plains Texas 1932, Ottumwa Iowa 1982, Renton Washington 1993, Bluff Creek Oct 20 1967, Madison Square Garden Jan 23 1984
3. **WHAT-IS-AT-STAKE** — the named conflict — first publicly-verified million in DK, the record-back-and-forth, debunking the surgeon's photo, beating the kill-screen
4. **THE DRAMATIC SPECIFIC** — the cool named detail in the stem — pizza-slice origin, taiko drums + chanting voices, the proximity-mines lobby, the ABACABB code, the Geinoh Yamashirogumi chorus
5. **THE PAYOFF (the answer)** — the most memorable specific cool fact — the iconic name / score / glitch / line / module / weapon / direct quote

If any of those five facts is missing or has been deferred to context, rewrite the stem.
