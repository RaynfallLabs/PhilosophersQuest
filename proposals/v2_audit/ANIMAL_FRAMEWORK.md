# Animal Bank Framework (2026-05-22)

This is the rule set for what a good Philosopher's Quest *animal* question looks like. The bank teaches fact + wonder — not name-recall, not definition lookup, not feel-good environmentalism. If a generation pass drifts from these rules, this document is why.

## Core principle

**Wonder + true fact.** A 5th-10th grader should leave this bank with:
- Bizarre, true, quotable animal facts they didn't know before (and that adults usually don't either)
- An understanding of *why* adaptations exist — environment → pressure → behavior/morphology
- The ability to read ecosystems as connected systems
- Western stewardship tradition (Roosevelt, Leopold, North American Model, Aquinas-Christian dominion-as-stewardship) as the legitimate framework for human-animal relations
- The ability to talk about dinosaurs, ice-age megafauna, deep-sea life, tropical biomes, parasites, convergent evolution, extremophiles with specificity

If a question can be answered by a toddler who knows common animal names ("striped African horse = zebra"), it has failed. If a question reveals something surprising and true that the player will *want to repeat to a friend*, it has succeeded.

## What this framework forbids

1. **Toddler-obvious common-name lookups** — "What is a striped African horse called?", "What is the king of the jungle?", "An African mammal with a long neck is a?", "A baby cat is called a?", "A group of wolves is a?". If a 4-year-old gets it from the cartoon, it does not belong in the bank.
2. **Definition recall** — "What does 'predator' mean?" / "What does 'biome' mean?" is vocabulary, not biology.
3. **Pure date recall** — "In what year did the dodo go extinct?" The May-19 audit dropped 21 of these; they must not creep back.
4. **Latin-name-as-test** — "What is the binomial for the polar bear?" Naming-as-trivia. Latin names belong in *context*, never as the test.
5. **AKC / kennel club categorizations** — "The Border Collie is in which AKC group?" These were dropped May-19; they remain banned. Working-dog *behavior* and *history* are fine; classification trivia is not.
6. **Yes/no with the obvious answer** — "Do bats fly? Yes." No question.
7. **Pure famous-animal name lookup** — "Cher Ami was famously the animal of?" Trivia. Use the bird's *story* instead, with the conclusion as the answer.
8. **Animal-rights moralism as default** — "Is it wrong to eat meat? Yes" / "Is hunting cruel? Yes." The bank does NOT moralize at the player against legitimate Western traditions of husbandry, hunting, and stewardship. See moral vision below.

## What the framework REQUIRES

1. **Wonder hook** — every question reveals something surprising. The stem either:
   - Reverses a common-knowledge assumption ("Zebras have stripes. What color is their base coat under the stripes?")
   - Names a specific bizarre behavior or capability
   - Shows an adaptive logic chain
   - Compares two species at a non-obvious feature
   - Tracks an ecological cascade
2. **Real biology** — every claim must be factually true. The bank goes through a factual-accuracy judgment gate.
3. **Forcing constraint** — the stem includes the detail that picks out exactly one correct answer.
4. **Steelman distractors** — wrong, but plausibly-wrong. A reader thinking "I see why someone might pick that" should be common.

## Where Latin names belong

**In the `context` field.** Always. Context is the place to say "*Hippotigris quagga* (the plains zebra)" or "this defense mechanism is named after the Greek *bombos* meaning rumble." That builds biological literacy without making the quiz itself name-recall.

## The five tiers

Each tier raises the **biological** depth, not just the reading level.

### T1 — 5th grade (10-11)

- **Scope**: bizarre-but-true wonder facts. Common-knowledge flips. Concrete examples a kid can picture.
- **Voice**: "Did you know?" energy. Concrete nouns. No Latin names in stem.
- **Content**: surprising senses (sharks smelling one drop of blood in a pool), unusual hunting behaviors (archerfish spitting water), unexpected colors (flamingos pink because of shrimp), beak/foot/skin specializations.
- **Form**: ≤200 char stem, ≤90 char each choice, ≤200 char context.
- **What NOT to ask**: any common-knowledge animal name. Any group/baby name a typical kid already knows (calf, kitten, foal, puppy, pack, flock, school, herd, swarm of bees, colony of ants).
- **What IS fine at T1**: non-obvious group names (*murder* of crows, *parliament* of owls), non-obvious baby names (*kit* for beaver/skunk, *joey* for kangaroo IF framed as a surprise).

### T2 — 6th grade (11-12)

- **Scope**: specific adaptations, hunting/mating displays, defense mechanisms, mimicry.
- **Voice**: short narrative + fact. Can introduce "predator", "prey", "habitat", "camouflage" without inline def.
- **Content**: octopus skin color-change, mantis shrimp punch, archerfish, peacock spider mating dance, snowshoe hare seasonal coat, Komodo dragon hunting, leaf-cutter ant fungus farming.
- **Form**: ≤240 char stem, ≤110 char each choice, ≤240 char context.

### T3 — 7th grade (12-13)

- **Scope**: ecology — niche partitioning, symbiosis, food webs, biome-specific creatures.
- **Voice**: wondrous observer + naturalist vocab ("niche", "biome", "trophic level", "symbiosis").
- **Content**: cleaner-fish mutualism, trophic cascades (sea otter → kelp → urchin), keystone species, convergent evolution (sugar glider vs flying squirrel), brood parasitism (cuckoo), extremophiles (tube worms at hydrothermal vents).
- **Form**: ≤280 char stem, ≤130 char each choice, ≤300 char context.

### T4 — 8th grade (13-14)

- **Scope**: evolutionary mechanisms (selection, drift, exaptation), conservation success/failure stories, intro to Western stewardship tradition.
- **Voice**: scientist-grade analytical. Can use "selection pressure", "fitness", "exaptation", "Hardy-Weinberg" with inline def.
- **Content**: Galápagos finches, peppered moth, antibiotic resistance, transitional fossils (Tiktaalik, Pakicetus), conservation case studies (American bison recovery, Pittman-Robertson Act, ESA Section 7), Aldo Leopold's land ethic introduced.
- **Form**: ≤320 char stem, ≤160 char each choice, ≤360 char context.

### T5 — 9-10th grade (14-16)

- **Scope**: deep ecology, animal cognition research, niche specialist facts, Western moral tradition on stewardship at scholarly depth.
- **Voice**: naturalist + ethicist. Specialist terms (phylogenetics, mutualism, fitness, kin selection, eusociality) with inline def where introduced.
- **Content**: corvid theory of mind, octopus cognition, cetacean culture, Hamilton's rule, eusocial reproductive suppression, North American Model of Wildlife Conservation as a substantive framework, Roosevelt's writings, Leopold's *Sand County Almanac*, hunters-fund-conservation mechanism, Aquinas/Aristotle/Christian stewardship as legitimate intellectual heritage.
- **Form**: ≤360 char stem, ≤180 char each choice, ≤420 char context.

## Substantive moral vision (T4-T5)

Drawn from [PHILOSOPHY_FRAMEWORK.md substantive moral vision section] and [feedback-moral-vision-substantive memory], applied to animals:

**1. Hunting is a legitimate Western tradition, not moral failing.**
- Roosevelt's North American Model: hunters fund conservation via excise taxes (Pittman-Robertson, Dingell-Johnson), regulated harvest sustains populations, public ownership of wildlife
- Aldo Leopold's land ethic: humans inside the system as stewards, not extracted observers
- Aquinas / Christian dominion = stewardship, not exploitation
- Hunting populations bottoming out under exclusion-only "preserves"; recovering under regulated-use models (whitetail deer, wild turkey, elk, bighorn sheep)

**2. Animal welfare ≠ animal rights.**
- Humane treatment of working/food animals is the substantive Western tradition
- Singer-style "speciesism" / animal rights coded views appear as positions to scrutinize, NOT as default
- The bank does not moralize at the player about meat-eating, fur, leather, working dogs, draft animals

**3. Conservation succeeds through use, not exclusion.**
- Substantive content on Pittman-Robertson (1937), Dingell-Johnson (1950), Migratory Bird Treaty Act (1918), Endangered Species Act mechanism
- Beaver, deer, elk, turkey, bison recoveries via regulated harvest + habitat management
- The exclusion-only approach is a position to scrutinize, not the bank's default

**4. Wonder over politics.**
- The vast majority of the bank is biological wonder, not human-animal politics
- Cultural/moral content lives at T4-T5 only, and there to anchor the kid in real Western tradition

**5. Reproduction in non-human species celebrated on its own terms.**
- Per moral-vision memory: never analogical commentary on human biology
- Clownfish protandry, wrasse protogyny, parthenogenesis in whiptail lizards / komodo dragons / sharks, *Schizophyllum commune* mating types — wonder on its own terms

**6. Honest about predation, death, parasitism.**
- The bank doesn't sanitize the natural world. Cats kill billions of songbirds. Cuckoos exploit foster parents. Parasitic wasps lay eggs inside living hosts. *Toxoplasma* manipulates rat behavior. These are biological wonders, not horror — but they're real.

## Wonder discipline

Every question must reveal something surprising. The wonder vocabulary the bank can pull from:

- **Sensory wonders**: shark electroreception, mantis-shrimp 16-color vision, snake heat pits, dog scent across 10,000 ft, bee UV vision, platypus electroreception
- **Mechanical wonders**: archerfish hydraulic spit, mantis-shrimp 50-mph punch, peregrine 240-mph dive, hummingbird hover, gecko van-der-Waals adhesion, snake jaw articulation
- **Chemical wonders**: bombardier beetle 100°C spray, fire salamander toxin, blue-ringed octopus tetrodotoxin, Komodo bacterial saliva mythology vs venom reality
- **Cooperative wonders**: leaf-cutter ant fungus farms, honeyguide-human partnerships, cleaner wrasse stations, ravens leading wolves to carcasses
- **Bizarre wonders**: tardigrade survival in space, axolotl regeneration, immortal jellyfish *Turritopsis*, hagfish slime, lungfish estivation
- **Group / baby names (NON-obvious only)**: murmuration of starlings, parliament of owls, embarrassment of pandas, siege of cranes, prickle of porcupines, kit (beaver, skunk, fox), porcupette, joey, puggle (echidna), eyas (hawk)
- **Group / baby names BANNED (toddler-level)**: pack of wolves, school of fish, herd of cows/sheep, flock of birds (generic), swarm of bees, colony of ants, calf, kitten, puppy, foal, lamb, kid (goat), chick, duckling

## Stance preserved (carry forward)

- Western tradition of stewardship, husbandry, hunting, conservation = legitimate inheritance
- Aldo Leopold, Theodore Roosevelt, Aquinas, Aristotle, North American Model treated as substantive intellectual figures
- Animal-rights coded views (Singer, PETA-aligned) included as positions to scrutinize, not as defaults
- Honest about ecological complexity (extinction is real; recovery is real; predation is real; cats killing songbirds is real)
- Christian-Crusader game framework: questions on animals in the Bible / Christian heritage are fine; no pantheonic animal-deity worship framings

## Implementation pattern

Every generated question goes through this pipeline:

```python
def q(tier, stem, choices, answer, context):
    cap = TIER_CAPS[tier]
    assert len(stem) <= cap['stem']
    for c in choices:
        assert len(c) <= cap['choice']
    assert len(context) <= cap['context']
    # Length parity
    lens = [len(c) for c in choices]
    assert max(lens) / min(lens) <= 1.30
    # Banned phrasings: toddler common-name lookups
    assert not TODDLER_PATTERNS.search(stem)
    # Wonder check: stem has behavior verb OR adaptation OR specific number/feature
    assert WONDER_DETECTOR.matches(stem)
    return {tier, question, choices, answer, context}
```

Plus the 8 deterministic gates carried from philosophy (adapted) + 4 judgment gates (especially factual_accuracy).

## How to use this framework

Future bank rebuilds: read this document FIRST. Then ANIMAL_TEMPLATES.md for concrete patterns. Then the moral vision memories. Generators instantiate templates that conform to this framework. If a pass produces toddler-level questions, animal-rights moralizing, or pure name-recall, the pass is wrong on the framework — not just on the templates.
