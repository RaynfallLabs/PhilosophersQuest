## 8. Tier-shift artifact (informational)

The post-merge audit reports 349 questions whose strict FK/jargon score
suggests a different tier than I assigned. These are NOT engine failures —
all 1255 pass deterministic validation. They represent tension between:
- **Stem FK** (which scores length, syllable density, sentence count)
- **Pedagogical tier** (which I assigned based on conceptual difficulty)

For example, a T2 question like "Your sister types a long question to a
chatbot. The bot only sees so much text at once. What is this limit called?"
has FK≈3 (short sentences), but introduces "context window" — a 6th-grade
concept, not a 5th-grade one.

The 349 suggested shifts skew DOWN (T2→T1: 128, T3→T1: 33, T3→T2: 25,
T2→T3: 22, etc.). Applying them would inflate T1 past 350 and hollow out
T2-T3. I left tier assignments as-is to preserve pedagogical level.

The 16 UPWARD shifts (e.g., T2 → T4/T5: 24, T3 → T4/T5: 84, T4 → T5: 12)
deserve attention. These are questions whose stem complexity does exceed
the assigned tier. Spot-checking, most are budget-blocked at their current
tier (the question's content fits the budget, but a higher tier would also
fit). Leaving them is benign — they pass the engine's gates.
