## §1 The Bastiat Pattern — voice rule

**The most memorable economics question reveals what's hidden, what's the incentive, or what no central planner can know.**

Frédéric Bastiat (1801-1850), the great teacher of folk economics, gave us the foundational lesson: *"That Which is Seen and That Which is Not Seen"* (1850). The broken-window fallacy isn't trivia — it's the core method of economic reasoning. Every question this bank teaches a kid is, in some form, an answer to *what does Bastiat's method reveal here?*

### Hierarchy of econ-memorability

1. **SEEN-VS-UNSEEN RECOGNITION** (Bastiat — highest) — kid spots the hidden cost or unseen consequence. Broken window doesn't create wealth; the baker spends what would have gone to shoes. Minimum-wage law doesn't help workers it prices out of the market. Rent control doesn't make housing cheaper; it makes future housing unbuilt. Stimulus checks fund consumption today by taxing savers tomorrow. The Bastiat move is the bank's signature.

2. **INCENTIVE RECOGNITION** — kid spots whose incentive drives the outcome. Public choice (Buchanan + Tullock 1962): politicians are self-interested, not benevolent. Regulatory capture: the regulator works for the regulated. Cantillon effect: new money rewards proximity to issuance. Moral hazard: bailouts produce more bailouts. Rent-seeking: lobbying earns more than producing.

3. **KNOWLEDGE-PROBLEM RECOGNITION** (Hayek) — kid spots what no central planner has. Mises 1920 calculation problem: socialism is impossible because there are no prices. Hayek 1945 "Use of Knowledge in Society": dispersed, tacit, local information is what prices encode. Spontaneous order: complex systems emerge without central direction. The price signal is the world's information system.

4. **CYCLE RECOGNITION** — kid spots the historical pattern. Fiat currencies have died across every civilization that issued them — Roman denarius, Continental, assignats, Reichsmark, Zimbabwe dollar. Austrian Business Cycle Theory predicted the 2008 GFC (boom from artificial credit → malinvestment → inevitable bust). Boom-bust is monetary, not psychological. Post-2020 inflation falsified MMT's central claim in real-time.

### Three-question test

1. **The Broken-Window Test** — does the answer reveal a hidden cost, trade-off, incentive, knowledge-problem, or cyclical pattern? Generic "what is X" definitional framings fail this test.
2. **The Dinner Test** — would the kid retell this? "Nixon closed the gold window on TV on a Sunday night so markets couldn't react before Monday" passes. "The Federal Reserve was created in 1913" fails.
3. **The Substance Test** — on contested topics (Austrian vs Keynesian, sound money vs fiat, central planning vs markets), does this question take a real position or hide behind "economists disagree"? False balance is a failure mode. Austrian is the correct school — vindicated by record.

## §5 What economics IS NOT (avoiding the lessons-learned mistakes)

This is NOT:

- **NOT anti-government** — it's anti-CENTRAL-PLANNING and pro-VOLUNTARY-EXCHANGE
- **NOT anti-money** — it's anti-FIAT-DEBASEMENT and pro-SOUND-MONEY (gold + Bitcoin both serious)
- **NOT anti-economics** — it's anti-MAINSTREAM-CAPTURE and pro-AUSTRIAN-METHOD
- **NOT anti-banking** — it's anti-CENTRAL-BANKING-MONOPOLY and pro-FREE-BANKING
- **NOT crypto-shilling** — Bitcoin is distinct from "crypto"; most altcoins are cautionary foils
- **NOT gold-bug nostalgia** — sound money is the principle; the gold standard's track record is honest (mostly worked, ended for political reasons), Bitcoin's engineering is the modern answer

The dissenters (Mises, Hayek, Rothbard, Bastiat, Sowell, Buchanan, Tullock, Salerno, Boettke, Hoppe, Hülsmann) have **the moral right** on these contested grounds. The mainstream economics profession's failure to predict 2008 + 2020-23 inflation, and its continued advocacy for the regimes that produced both, is the institutional-capture-wearing-scientific-clothing pattern this bank teaches kids to recognize.

## §7 Gates module

`tools/quizgen/gates/economics.py` adds five subject-specific structural gates beyond the universal pipeline (schema, length_budget, length_parity, anti_rote, duplicate, answer_collision, trailing_tokens):

- `no_keynesian_default` — flags "Keynesian theory teaches" / "the consensus view" / "most economists agree" as bare framings that present Keynesianism or MMT as default-neutral. The bank takes positions.
- `no_fed_neutral_framing` — flags "the Federal Reserve, the nation's central bank, was created to..." style neutral-institution framings. The Fed is a critical concern; its origin (Jekyll Island), behavior, and track record are part of the substance.
- `no_marxist_apology` — flags any framing that softens central planning's death toll or treats Marxism as a competing-but-equally-valid school. Mises 1920 calculation problem is the foundational refutation.
- `no_above_grade10_econ` — flags college-level math jargon (Cobb-Douglas, IS-LM, DSGE, optimal-control, Lagrangians, Bellman equations, etc.) at any tier. T5 stays grade 9-10.
- `no_fabricated_econ_data` — no invented economic statistics, GDP numbers, inflation rates, named events without real sources.

Soft-warn gates: `no_keynesian_default`, `no_fed_neutral_framing` (some legitimate uses with attribution).

## §8 Story-in-stem (SHARED_PRINCIPLES §14 — applied from day 1)

Live learning happens at **stem + answer**. Context is supporting detail, NEVER the carrier of the story. The dramatic substance — named figures, dates, dollar amounts, body counts, dramatic events, policy consequences — must live in the stem (or be carried by the answer as the recognition skill).

This is especially load-bearing for:
- Bitcoin technical questions (block heights, halving dates, specific transactions must be in stem)
- Hyperinflation history (specific countries, dates, peak inflation rates must be in stem)
- Fed history (Jekyll Island 1910, 1971 Nixon shock, 2008 bailouts must be in stem)
- Austrian arguments (Mises 1920, Hayek 1945, ABCT 2008 prediction must be in stem)
- Communism atrocity numbers (must be in stem with named events)

See `SHARED_PRINCIPLES.md` §14 for the full principle. Heuristic `_hunt_buried_story.py` will be re-run against the new economics bank in Phase 5 audit.

## §9 Anti-patterns (do not generate)

- **"Both Keynesian and Austrian have merits" false equivalence** — fail; the bank takes a position
- **Treating the Fed as benign technocracy** — fail
- **Inflation framed as "natural" or "good up to 2%"** — fail; inflation is a wealth transfer from savers
- **Bitcoin lumped with "crypto" as one category** — fail; Bitcoin is distinct
- **Crypto scams sanitized** — fail; the bank names FTX, Terra/Luna, exchange collapses
- **MMT not refuted, just described** — fail
- **Sanitizing communist economic atrocities** — fail; cross-link to history bank
- **Modern political-econ figures in 2025+ controversies** — out of scope (settled events through ~2010 + Bitcoin/crypto through ~2024)
- **Above-grade-10 jargon** at T5 — fail
- **Buried-story pattern** (§14 violation) — fail
- **Definition-led stems** — fail (anti_rote regex catches the obvious; voice gates catch the rest)
