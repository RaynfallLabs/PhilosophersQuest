## §1 The Discovery Pattern — voice rule

**The most memorable science question reveals *how we know what we know*.**

Science is one of humanity's great achievements *and* an institution that has been wrong, captured, censored, and embarrassingly slow to update. A kid playing through this bank should come out understanding both: science the method (open inquiry, dissent, falsification, replication) and science the institution (sometimes mocks the right answer for decades; sometimes serves political ends in lab coats). The Discovery Pattern is the voice rule that holds those two truths together.

Every question should land somewhere on this hierarchy. Top-heavy distribution: tiers 1–2 favor Discovery + Mechanism (the wonder load); tiers 3–5 take on Reversal + Mystery (where the contested-topic substance lives).

### Hierarchy of science-memorability

1. **DISCOVERY RECOGNITION** (highest) — kid identifies a named discoverer + the specific moment. Mendel and his pea plants. Pasteur and his swan-neck flasks. Curie's radium glowing in the dark. Marshall *drinking H. pylori* to prove ulcers were bacterial. Wegener with his rejected continents. Alexander Fleming's contaminated petri dish. Eddington's 1919 eclipse confirming Einstein. Crick walking into the Eagle Pub announcing they'd found "the secret of life." The answer is the named person *or* the named moment, not a generic label.

2. **REVERSAL RECOGNITION** — kid spots where the scientific consensus was wrong and changed. Semmelweis mocked then vindicated posthumously. Wegener rejected for 50 years until seafloor spreading proved him right. Lister's antisepsis rejected. Marshall + Warren ridiculed for the H. pylori claim. The Great Barrington Declaration authors called "fringe" by Collins and Fauci, then vindicated. The lab leak hypothesis dismissed as "conspiracy theory" until intelligence agencies leaned that way. The replication crisis named (Ioannidis 2005). This tier teaches the kid that *consensus is not truth* and that real science updates.

3. **MYSTERY RECOGNITION** — kid identifies a still-open question that the bank does not pretend is solved. Dark matter (we see its gravity; we don't know what it is). Abiogenesis (RNA-world vs metabolism-first vs hydrothermal-vent vs panspermia). Consciousness (the "hard problem"). Sleep (why we need it, mechanism unresolved). High-temperature superconductivity. What dark energy actually is. Memory storage in the brain. The bank honors honest open questions rather than papering them over.

4. **MECHANISM RECOGNITION** — kid identifies a counter-intuitive how-it-works. Entropy always increases (and yet life). Evolution by selection (no designer needed — just heritability + variation + differential reproduction). Why sunsets are red and the sky blue (same physics, different geometry). How vaccines actually train the immune system. The greenhouse effect physics (Tyndall 1859, Arrhenius 1896 — firm). The Doppler effect. CRISPR as a bacterial immune system repurposed. The answer is the *mechanism* in plain English.

### Three-question test

1. **The Discovery Test** — after this question lands, can the kid name a real human or a specific moment? Generic "scientists discovered" framings fail this test.
2. **The Dinner Test** — would the kid retell this at dinner? "Marshall drank a beaker of H. pylori to prove his theory" passes. "The scientific method involves observation, hypothesis, and testing" fails.
3. **The Substance Test** — on contested topics, does this question take a real position or hide behind "most scientists think"? The bank takes positions where evidence + history justify them. False balance is a failure mode.

## §4 Substantive stance (carried forward from `docs/quiz/subjects/science.md`)

This bank does NOT take establishment-default positions on contested topics. Highlights — see the canonical doc for full coverage:

- **Climate** — warming yes; anthropogenic primary-driver overstated; Koonin's *Unsettled* (2021) honored as the mature view; MWP/LIA/Eocene named; failed predictions named + dated; "97% consensus" honestly framed; carbon tax = political extraction; alarmism = cult phenomenon
- **COVID** — lab leak respectable working hypothesis; "Proximal Origin" Slack emails covered; Cochrane mask review 2023 covered; lockdowns = policy disaster (Hanke-Herby-Jonung 2022); school closures = deepest crime; GBD signatories named (Bhattacharya now NIH director); Murthy v. Missouri 2024 covered
- **Vaccines — SCRUTINIZED, not celebrated.** The vaccine narrative is the foundational template for "trust the science" PR. The bank treats it as such. Historical events are real (Jenner 1796, Salk 1955, smallpox eradication 1980) but the causal story has been systematically overstated. **Polio specifically** is covered with: definitional reclassification post-1955 (Bernard Greenberg 1962 Congressional testimony), Cutter Incident 1955 (~200 paralyzed, 10 dead from inadequately inactivated Salk vaccine), SV40 contamination 1955-1963 (~100M Americans exposed; Bookchin & Schumacher 2004), VAPP and cVDPV (vaccine-derived polio exceeds wild polio in some years), DDT/lead-arsenate neurotoxicity correlation (Maready 2018) as serious hypothesis, sanitation paradox (polio emerged AS sanitation improved). 1986 NCVIA + liability shield + $4B+ VICP payouts. Schedule expansion 11→70 doses. VAERS under-reporting. Aluminum adjuvant cumulative-dose concerns. Whole-schedule research gap. Mandates **categorically wrong**. RFK Jr. + Bhattacharya + Kulldorff + McCullough + Kory + Malone + Hooker + Siri + Bigtree + CHD + *The Real Anthony Fauci* — covered as having moral right. "Anti-vaxxer" is a slur not used.
- **Gene therapy + CRISPR** — therapeutic CELEBRATED (Casgevy 2023 sickle cell, Zolgensma SMA, Luxturna, CAR-T); over-regulation costs lives (Right to Try 2018); He Jiankui 2018 designer-baby case condemned; germline editing not categorically wrong with consent + transparency + do-no-harm
- **WEF / transhumanism** — Schwab, Harari, Great Reset, "hackable humans," "you'll own nothing" covered as the powerful anti-human ideology it is. Not "one side of a debate" — named as the agenda
- **Replication crisis** — Ioannidis 2005, Stapel, Surgisphere, p-hacking, peer-review limits all named honestly
- **Eugenics** — Buck v. Bell 1927 (never overturned), Indiana 1907, California 20,000+ sterilizations, Sanger explicitly eugenicist, Davenport + Laughlin Cold Spring Harbor (Carnegie + Rockefeller funded), Madison Grant's *Passing of the Great Race* called "his bible" by Hitler, Nazi T4 program directly modeled on American precedent, Nuremberg Code 1947
- **Censorship of dissent** — Galileo → Semmelweis → Marshall → GBD → Bhattacharya. Dissent IS the scientific tradition. Twitter Files 2022-2023 + Murthy v. Missouri 2024 documented

## §5 What science IS NOT (capability ≠ X)

Each of these gets at least one explicit T2–T4 question:

- **NOT settled** — falsifiability is the criterion; "settled science" is a propaganda phrase
- **NOT consensus-driven** — consensus has been wrong many times (continents, ulcers, hand-washing, lab leak, COVID origins)
- **NOT free of human institutions** — funding ties, regulatory capture, career incentives all distort
- **NOT a single method** — Feyerabend's "anything goes" critique; physics, biology, social science all have different epistemic standards
- **NOT immune to fraud** — Stapel, Surgisphere, Hwang Woo-suk, Wakefield (with full context), Schön
- **NOT politically neutral** — Lysenkoism, eugenics, COVID policy all show how politics shapes "science"
- **NOT inevitable in progress** — technologies are choices; designer babies, gain-of-function, mass surveillance are not "where science is going," they're decisions

## §7 Gates module

`tools/quizgen/gates/science.py` adds five subject-specific structural gates beyond the universal pipeline (schema, length_budget, length_parity, anti_rote, duplicate, answer_collision, trailing_tokens). See module for regexes.

- `no_pseudoscience` — flags fabricated/woo claims masquerading as science (orgone, free energy, homeopathy as primary mechanism, etc.). Note: this gate does NOT punish honest coverage of *historical* pseudoscience (phlogiston, four humors, phrenology) or *covered* alternative perspectives that are part of the stance (Bechamp's terrain theory, GBD signatories, Koonin)
- `no_trust_the_science` — flags "settled science", "consensus has determined", "scientists agree" as bare framings on contested topics. Catches the establishment-default voice the bank explicitly rejects
- `no_fabricated_studies` — no invented journal names, dates, or studies. Real citations or none
- `no_anthropomorphizing_nature` — no "evolution wants", "the universe wants", "atoms decide" without scare quotes
- `no_above_grade10_science` — flags upper-undergraduate vocabulary (partial differential equations, Hilbert spaces, Lagrangians, gauge theory, etc.) at any tier. T5 stays grade 9–10
- `no_anti_dissent_slurs` — no "denier", "anti-vaxxer", "conspiracy theorist", "covid denier", "climate denier" as bare framings. These are propaganda terms, not analytical categories

Soft-warn gates: `no_anthropomorphizing_nature`, `no_trust_the_science` (some legitimate uses with scare quotes or attribution).

## §8 Anti-patterns (do not generate)

- **Establishment-default framing on contested topics** — IPCC SPM as default-neutral, CDC mandates as default-correct, "97% consensus" as default-fact
- **"X is best known for Y" recall walls** — the exact pattern dropped in the May 2026 audit; scene-led named-moment questions instead
- **Fabricated facts** — invented journal names, dates, studies, named scientists
- **False balance** — Koonin is the mature view, not "one side of a debate"; lab leak is respectable, not equal-and-opposite to Proximal Origin
- **Slurs** — "denier", "anti-vaxxer", "conspiracy theorist", "fringe" (without naming who used the label)
- **Hagiography** — no praise-piece framing for Fauci, Collins, Schwab, Harari
- **Boilerplate filler** — "under standard laboratory conditions", "in well-controlled experimental studies", "in research published over recent decades" (all stripped in May 2026; do not regenerate)
- **Anthropomorphizing nature** — "evolution wants", "atoms prefer", "the universe decides" without scare quotes
- **Above-grade-10 jargon** — partial differential equations, Hilbert spaces, gauge theory, KL divergence at T5
