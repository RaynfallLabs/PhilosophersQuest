# People: the kinds whose body is SEARCHED, never harvested (Brandon,
# 2026-11-16: "Humans are not harvested. That's weird, we're not cannibals").
# H on a person's body takes a couple of ordinary finds from what the person
# carried (data/cooking_rules.json "search"; food_system.search_corpse): no
# question, the body used up, like picking something up.
#
# Who is a person is decided by lore: humans and the human-like folk (elves,
# dwarves, halflings, gnomes, named people). Goblinoids, orcs, gnolls,
# kobolds, lizardfolk, giants, ogres and the undead stay as they were. The
# build writes this list to data/items/harvest_map.json "people"; a kind on it
# is in no kin group and yields no ingredient. A creature monsters.json does
# not know (Gilt Varro of the racing camp) is a person when its body says it
# is one of the mortals (kitchen.is_person).
#
# The calls on the kinds at the edge (each is in the report to Brandon):
#   * werewolf, werewolf_alpha, elder_werewolf, werebear, wererat, weretiger:
#     a beast-curse on a man, but Ovid's Lycaon stayed a wolf, and down here
#     they die as beasts. Harvested.
#   * yuan_ti_pureblood: serpent folk who only pass for human. Harvested.
#   * green_hag, sea_hag, annis_hag, night_hag: fey spirits of ponds and
#     nightmares (Jenny Greenteeth, the mare). Harvested.
#   * baba_yaga: the iron-toothed witch-spirit of the Russian tales; what she
#     gives is a spark from her oven, not flesh. Harvested.
#   * green_knight: an enchanted giant of the fey; his trophy is his holly
#     bough. Harvested.
#   * erlking: Goethe's elf-king is a death spirit in the mist; his part is
#     a mist-acorn. Harvested.
#   * leprechaun, leprechaun_hostile, spriggan, redcap, boggart, changeling,
#     puca, satyr_warrior, satyr_trickster, pixie_swarm, wild_hunt_captain:
#     fairies and spirits, not folk; their parts are glows and sparks.
#     Harvested.
#   * old_nickel: a kobold (the miners' spite of the deep shafts). Harvested.
#   * tengu_warrior, sobek_warrior, kusarikku, deep_one_priest, lamia, medusa
#     and the gorgons, harpy: beast-folk of myth. Harvested.
#   * wendigo, neferkaptah_risen, death_mage and the undead necromancers:
#     undead. Harvested (Brandon's rule for the undead).
#   * necromancer_apprentice: a living man who has only begun the art.
#     A person.
#   * einherjar: Odin's chosen dead, whole again in Valhalla, who ride out of
#     Gjallarhorn's call. A person (his ghost, einherjar_ghost, stays a ghost).
#   * sown_warrior: Cadmus's men grown from the dragon's teeth. A person.
#   * mind_flayer_thrall: a scholar or a soldier once, his mind taken. A person.
#   * dark_elf, drow_*, light_elf_hostile, huldufolk_raider: elves and the
#     hidden folk. People. nibelung_hoardwarden: a dwarf. A person.
