- **Drifting continents and deep time (lead's ruling, 2026-11-26).** Five units share this ground. Who owns what:
  - `plates-at-work` (T3 earth) owns the plate process: the boundary types and what each makes, hot spots, mantle currents as the driver, and where earthquakes and volcanoes cluster.
    - It does not ask what convection is; `heat-on-the-move` asks that.
    - It does not ask about Tharp, the magnetic stripes, or how old the sea floor is; those belong to Wegener and T5.
  - `fossils-layers-and-a-changing-earth` (T3 earth) owns the evidence itself:
    - sea fossils high on mountains;
    - Glossopteris and the reptiles across the southern continents;
    - coal in Antarctica;
    - matching rocks and mountain belts;
    - reading a cliff (oldest layer at the bottom; bent or broken after they formed).
  - `wegener-and-the-drifting-continents` (T3 method) owns the story:
    - the jigsaw fit and Pangaea;
    - why geologists rejected him (no force he could name);
    - Tharp's maps and the magnetic stripes;
    - how the evidence changed minds.
    - It does not re-ask Glossopteris or the coal as evidence. It may ask what the critics said those fossils showed instead (land bridges).
  - `reading-deep-time` (T3 method) owns the method:
    - Steno as the person behind superposition (not "oldest at the bottom" again);
    - relative vs absolute age;
    - index fossils, William Smith, Hutton;
    - the time scale and Earth's 4.6 billion years.
  - `earths-interior-and-age` (T5 earth) owns:
    - sea-floor ages from the ridges outward;
    - earthquake waves and the core;
    - radiometric clocks and Patterson.
- **Greenhouse ownership (lead's ruling, 2026-11-26).** The why-earth judge flagged that `the-greenhouse-effect` (T4) lists ground this T3 unit touches.
  - `why-earth-supports-life` (T3) keeps the habitability side:
    - Earth would average about -18 C with no greenhouse gases, 33 degrees colder (2fe68de4a8);
    - Venus's air holds vastly more carbon dioxide than Mars's (2cf96b1e28);
    - most of Earth's carbon dioxide is locked in rocks such as limestone (b25bf74134), told as why Earth is not Venus.
  - `the-greenhouse-effect` (T4) owns the physics:
    - the mechanism itself (the ground gives off infrared, the gases absorb it and send part back), with Tyndall's measurement;
    - Arrhenius, Keeling, the other carbon stores, and volcanic cooling.
    - Its brief must say it may not re-ask the -18 C or the limestone store; it asks oceans, living things and fossil fuels as stores instead.
  - So the T3 mechanism question cb28d1ffb6 (absorb and send part back) is T4's. The T3 reviser drops it, or replaces it with another habitability fact in scope.
- **Experiment design ladder (lead's ruling, 2026-11-26).** Several method units share this ground.
  - T1 `measuring-and-the-fair-test` keys the fair test itself: change one thing, keep the rest the same, repeat.
  - `designing-a-controlled-experiment` (T3) owns:
    - naming the independent, dependent and controlled variables in a described experiment;
    - what a control group is for;
    - spotting a flaw (two things changed at once, no control);
    - what a result does and does not show.
    - It may ask why many trials or a large sample help, but only inside a described design (one odd plant, chance), never "repeat the trials" again.
    - It does not key Lind (T5 `fair-trials-controls-and-placebos`), blinding or placebos (T4 `testing-a-claim`, T5), or correlation (T4).
  - `correlation-causation-and-error` (T4) owns precision vs accuracy, measurement error, survivorship bias and correlation. It must not re-ask "more trials help".
- **Equations ladder (lead's ruling, 2026-11-26).**
  - T3 `elements-compounds-and-formulas` keys reading a formula: how many atoms of each element are in H2O or CO2.
  - `chemical-reactions-conserve-mass` (T4) owns:
    - atoms rearranged, never made or destroyed;
    - Lavoisier's sealed vessels;
    - mass that seems to vanish when a gas escapes, or seems to appear when a gas joins (rust, a burning metal);
    - reading a balanced equation that is given: what the arrow means, what a number in front means, counting atoms on each side.
    - It must not ask how to balance one (choosing the numbers).
    - It should not use 2H2 + O2 -> 2H2O, which is T5's example.
  - `balancing-equations-and-reaction-rates` (T5) owns balancing an equation, the kinds of reaction (combustion, oxidation, neutralisation, acid on metal) and what speeds a reaction.
  - `photosynthesis-atoms-in-and-out` (T4 life) owns where a tree's mass comes from (van Helmont's willow, carbon from the air). The mass unit may say a burning log's mass goes into the air as gas, but must not key the tree's mass.

- **Genetics ladder (lead's ruling, 2026-11-26).**
  - T3 `mendels-peas-and-inherited-traits` owns Mendel's crosses, dominant and recessive, and Punnett squares.
